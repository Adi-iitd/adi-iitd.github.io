R. Gupta, A. Kumar, S. Chaudhury, B. Lall, and V. Kaushik
IEEE: 2020 National Conference on Communications, 2020

Sampling considers the global context of the image, and a DNN is trained to reconstruct from compressed measurements. Project Details

🔍 Search and Recommendation System

Production-grade hybrid search engine with semantic understanding

  • Addressed poor search performance in a consumer kitchen assistant app serving 700-800 daily queries by designing and deploying a production-grade hybrid search engine combining BM25 with GIST-Embedding-v0 semantic models across 1,000 recipes and 200 ingredients. Overcame lack of real training data due to missing user logs by training custom domain-specific embedding models using synthetic datasets generated through LLM-based augmentation and classical NLP techniques (character swaps, Levenshtein ≤3), enabling semantic understanding of ingredient substitutions, recipe variations, and Hindi-English code-mixed queries.
  • Solved poor fuzzy query handling by building a robust multilingual query correction system trained on synthetic spelling variants and transliteration patterns, enabling accurate retrieval for misspelled or multilingual inputs in Hindi and English. Eliminated slow search latency by optimizing infrastructure with FAISS binary quantized vectors for sub-20ms candidate generation, followed by full-precision re-ranking with personalized user embeddings, maintaining Elasticsearch-comparable performance while adding semantic capabilities.
  • Delivered measurable business impact through 30% improvement in NDCG@5 for exact matches, 20% increase in recipe conversion rate (A/B tested), and 70% retrieval success for previously unsupported fuzzy queries (compared to 0% baseline).
  • Extended the trained semantic embeddings to power personalized recipe recommendations, combining content-based filtering (user preferences, dietary tags, cooking history) with collaborative filtering from similar users' behavior to deliver relevant suggestions for returning users. Solved the cold-start problem for new users with no cooking history by introducing an onboarding flow: users answer a short questionnaire (e.g., allergens, dietary habits, cuisine preferences, region) and select 3+ recipes from a tailored list. This information is used to build an initial user profile that powers content-based recommendations from day one—similar to onboarding flows in Netflix and Spotify.

🍲 Universal Frying Model (UFM)

Swin Transformer regression model for real-time doneness prediction

  • Eliminated inconsistent cooking outcomes caused by fixed-timer failures by developing a Swin Transformer regression model that predicts ingredient doneness on a continuous 0–1 scale using real-time visual cues. Solved training data scarcity by designing a bucketed data generation pipeline with labeled intervals (0.25–0.75) across lighting conditions, and synthesizing realistic multi-ingredient scenes via area-weighted label mixing with adjacent-bucket constraints.
  • Achieved real-time inference by deploying a monitoring loop that processes raw camera frames every 10 seconds with no preprocessing, delivering ~30ms latency on cloud and ~100ms on edge devices during active frying. Demonstrated strong generalization across diverse conditions with 0.025 MAE on validation data from disjoint sessions and unseen lighting environments.
  • Improved recipe quality by dynamically adapting frying duration to actual visual progression, eliminating timing-based failures and ensuring consistent results across ingredient variations and user prep styles.

🥘 Food and Ingredient Segmentation with Dispense Localization

Real-time SegFormer pipeline for ingredient detection and classification

  • Addressed unreliable ingredient detection in cluttered kitchen environments by developing a real-time SegFormer segmentation pipeline for pan, food, robotic arms, and background detection, trained on 1,500 annotated images across challenging conditions (occlusion, glare, low light). Overcame limited training data constraints by implementing self-distillation using 2,000 pseudo-labeled frames with ≥95% confidence, achieving 97% mean IoU with a +1% gain from the expanded dataset.
  • Solved ingredient identification challenges in mixed food scenarios by building a dispense localization module triggered 2 seconds after each ingredient addition (max 4 times per recipe), achieving 90% IoU with a +3.5% improvement from distillation-based training. Improved ingredient classification by integrating Swin Transformer on localized regions, achieving 85% accuracy and 82% F1 score compared to sub-60% accuracy on full-pan images.

🕵 Vision-Based Quality Auditors for Robotic Cooking

YOLOv8 and SegFormer systems for automated quality control

  • Prevented unreliable predictions from degraded visual inputs by developing a YOLOv8 image quality checker as first-stage validation, trained on 5,000 edge-case images to detect blur, poor lighting, and occlusions, achieving 92.5% IoU and 99.2% F1 score. Implemented automated failure recovery by pausing recipe execution after 5 consecutive bad frames and prompting user camera cleaning, preventing cascading errors in downstream vision models.
  • Addressed splatter buildup in gravy-heavy recipes by building a SegFormer-based splatter detection system trained on 500 annotated images, achieving 70% IoU and automatically increasing stirrer speed to 90% when splatter exceeds 1% threshold.
  • Eliminated food safety risks from undercooked lumps by implementing segmentation-based lump detection for meat (82% IoU) and noodles (88% IoU), triggering automated interventions (100ml water for noodles, 50ml water + re-stirring for meat) to replicate human corrective actions.

COUNTERTOP COOKING ROBOT
PCT/US2024/061909
Issued Jul 3, 2025

A countertop cooking appliance that uses machine learning models to automatically prepare a plurality of different meals is described. The appliance includes a micro dispensing system containing a plurality of pods having granular contents. The pod rotation mechanism moves a selected pod into a position above a pan to dispense the granular contents. The rotation element rotates the selected pod to dispense an amount of granular content from the dispensing section with each rotation of the selected pod by the rotation element. The appliance may also include a stirrer that uses at least one spatula to gradually contact substantially an entire area of the pan after the stirrer completes a rotation cycle. Control circuitry coupled to a macro ingredient delivery system, the micro dispensing system, the stirrer, a heating element, and sensors performs recipe methods using a plurality of computer vision models to monitor recipe progress.
